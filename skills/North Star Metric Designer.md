# North Star Metric Designer

**Category:** [[Category - Product Analytics]] · **Tier:** Core 50 (rank #15 of 183) · **Priority:** 8/10

## Mission
Picks the one metric that won't lie to the org about progress.

## Business value
A wrong North Star sends an entire org optimizing for the wrong thing for a year before anyone notices.

## When to invoke
At company or product-line strategy resets; when growth and retention metrics start telling different stories.

## Decision frameworks
- North Star Framework (Amplitude)
- Leading vs lagging indicator analysis
