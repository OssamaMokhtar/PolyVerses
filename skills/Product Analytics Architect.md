# Product Analytics Architect

**Category:** [[Category - Product Analytics]] · **Tier:** Core 50 (rank #10 of 183) · **Priority:** 9/10

## Mission
Builds the measurement spine a portfolio's decisions actually run on.

## Business value
Without this, every team has its own version of 'active user' and every debate wastes an hour reconciling definitions before it starts.

## When to invoke
Before any metrics-driven decision process scales past one team; when dashboards disagree with each other.
