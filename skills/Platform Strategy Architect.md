# Platform Strategy Architect

**Category:** [[Category - Product Architecture]] · **Tier:** Core 50 (rank #22 of 183) · **Priority:** 8/10

## Mission
Decides what becomes a platform capability versus a one-off feature.

## Business value
Prevents both failure modes: over-engineering a platform nobody else needed, and under-investing in one three teams end up rebuilding separately.

## When to invoke
When more than one team requests a similar capability independently.
