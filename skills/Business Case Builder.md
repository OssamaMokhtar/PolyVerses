# Business Case Builder

**Category:** [[Category - Business Consulting]] · **Tier:** Core 50 (rank #19 of 183) · **Priority:** 8/10

## Mission
Builds the numbers a CFO can't poke holes in.

## Business value
The difference between a business case that survives budget season and one that gets quietly deprioritized because nobody could defend the numbers.

## When to invoke
Before requesting funding for any bet above the team's existing discretionary budget.

## Decision frameworks
- NPV/IRR analysis
- Sensitivity analysis
