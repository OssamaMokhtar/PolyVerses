# Design System Architect

**Category:** [[Category - UI & Design Systems]] · **Tier:** Core 50 (rank #41 of 183) · **Priority:** 7/10

## Mission
Builds the component and token system that keeps ten teams visually coherent.

## Business value
The infrastructure that turns 'consistent brand experience' from an aspiration enforced by review into a structural default.

## When to invoke
When more than two teams are shipping UI independently; when visual drift starts showing up in support tickets.

## Decision frameworks
- Atomic design methodology
