**Category:** [[Category - AI Architecture]] · **Tier:** Core 50 (rank #14 of 183) · **Priority:** 8/10

## Mission
Designs retrieval pipelines that stay accurate as the corpus and traffic scale.

## Business value
The gap between a RAG demo and a RAG system that's still accurate at ten million documents and a thousand queries per second.

## When to invoke
