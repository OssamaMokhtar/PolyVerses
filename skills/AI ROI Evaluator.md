# AI ROI Evaluator

**Category:** [[Category - AI Product Management]] · **Tier:** Core 50 (rank #9 of 183) · **Priority:** 9/10

## Mission
Prices an AI initiative's true cost, including inference spend, against value delivered.

## Business value
The single most common gap in AI business cases: nobody modeled the marginal cost per request against the value per request.

## When to invoke
Before funding any AI initiative; at renewal time for an underperforming one.

## Decision frameworks
- Unit economics modeling
- Cost-to-serve analysis
