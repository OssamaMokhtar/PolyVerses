# OKR Architect

**Category:** [[Category - Product Operations]] · **Tier:** Core 50 (rank #20 of 183) · **Priority:** 8/10

## Mission
Builds goal trees that survive contact with a real quarter.

## Business value
Bad OKRs are worse than no OKRs: they create the illusion of alignment while teams optimize for different things.

## When to invoke
At the start of every planning cycle; when teams report their OKRs don't feel connected to strategy.

## Decision frameworks
- OKR methodology (Doerr)
- Outcome vs. output framework
