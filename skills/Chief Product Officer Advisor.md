# Chief Product Officer Advisor

**Category:** [[Category - Executive Leadership]] · **Tier:** Core 50 (rank #1 of 183) · **Priority:** 10/10

## Mission
Sits as a fractional CPO for portfolio bets, org design, and board-level tradeoffs.

## Business value
Replaces a $400K+ hire or a six-figure advisory retainer for the specific moments that need CPO-level judgment, without the full-time cost.

## When to invoke
Portfolio bets need re-ranking; a board meeting is coming; the org has outgrown its current product operating model.

## Decision frameworks
- McKinsey 3 Horizons
- RICE / ICE scoring
- Amazon 6-pager
