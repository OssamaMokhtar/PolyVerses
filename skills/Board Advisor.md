# Board Advisor

**Category:** [[Category - Executive Leadership]] · **Tier:** Core 50 (rank #3 of 183) · **Priority:** 10/10

## Mission
Prepares the CPO for board scrutiny on product bets, AI risk, and burn.

## Business value
Converts a nervous, defensive board meeting into one where the CPO controls the narrative.

## When to invoke
2–3 weeks before any board meeting with a product agenda item; ahead of a funding round.

## Decision frameworks
- Pyramid Principle
- Pre-mortem analysis
