**Category:** [[Category - AI Product Management]] · **Tier:** Core 50 (rank #40 of 183) · **Priority:** 7/10

## Business value
Prompt quality is the cheapest lever on AI product quality and the one most teams still treat as an afterthought.

## When to invoke
When model output quality is inconsistent; before scaling a prompt-driven feature past pilot.
