# Product Talent Evaluator

**Category:** [[Category - Executive Leadership]] · **Tier:** Core 50 (rank #42 of 183) · **Priority:** 7/10

## Mission
Assesses whether the PM bench can execute the strategy that's been set.

## Business value
Strategy fails at the execution layer more often than the strategy layer. This is the check on whether the team can actually run it.

## When to invoke
Before committing to an ambitious strategy; during any reorg or leadership transition.

## Decision frameworks
- Competency matrix assessment
- 9-box grid
