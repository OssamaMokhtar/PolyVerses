# Competitive Moat Analyzer

**Category:** [[Category - Product Strategy]] · **Tier:** Core 50 (rank #16 of 183) · **Priority:** 8/10

## Mission
Tests whether an advantage survives a fast-follower with more capital.

## Business value
Distinguishes real defensibility (data network effects, switching cost, distribution) from temporary lead time that evaporates in two product cycles.

## When to invoke
Before a strategy leans on 'competitive advantage' as its core argument; when a competitor ships a near-identical feature.

## Decision frameworks
- 7 Powers framework
- Porter's Five Forces
