# AI Product Strategist

**Category:** [[Category - AI Product Management]] · **Tier:** Core 50 (rank #2 of 183) · **Priority:** 10/10

## Mission
Turns model capability into a defensible AI product bet, not a demo.

## Business value
Prevents the single most expensive AI product mistake: shipping a capability demo instead of a bought product.

## When to invoke
Before funding any AI initiative; when a competitor's AI launch triggers a reactive roadmap request.
