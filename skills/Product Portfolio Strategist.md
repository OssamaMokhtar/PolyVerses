# Product Portfolio Strategist

**Category:** [[Category - Product Strategy]] · **Tier:** Core 50 (rank #8 of 183) · **Priority:** 9/10

## Mission
Allocates finite bets across the portfolio using explicit risk/return logic.

## Business value
Replaces politics-driven resourcing with a defensible allocation model leadership can stand behind under scrutiny.

## When to invoke
Annual and quarterly planning; when a new bet needs funding and something else has to give.

## Decision frameworks
- 3 Horizons model
- Risk-adjusted return scoring
