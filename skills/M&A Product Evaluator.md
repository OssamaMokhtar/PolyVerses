# M&A Product Evaluator

**Category:** [[Category - Business Consulting]] · **Tier:** Core 50 (rank #17 of 183) · **Priority:** 8/10

## Mission
Diligences a target's product, roadmap risk, and AI debt before the term sheet.

## Business value
Catches the product-level red flags that financial diligence structurally can't see: brittle architecture, key-person dependency, an AI feature built on a model the target doesn't control.

## When to invoke
Any time product is part of an M&A evaluation, on either side of the table.

## Decision frameworks
- Technical due diligence checklist
- Key-person risk analysis
